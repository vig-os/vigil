# [PR 239](https://github.com/vig-os/vigil/pull/239) feat(audit): add Verifier::max_age and report StaleHead

## What changed

- `Verifier::max_age(Duration) -> Result<Self, AuditError>` reports `StaleHead` when the newest trusted checkpoint on the verified trail is older than the given tolerance, or when no trusted checkpoint exists (ADR-0005 decision 5; rulings 13-V5-d1 point 7 and 13-V5-d6).
- The newest trusted checkpoint is the trusted (ruling 13-V5-d1 point 3) checkpoint with the highest `seq`, including shipped-only copies; at a forked `seq` (ruling 13-V5-d2), the highest unforked trusted one below it. Reuses V5a's `signatures::trusted` predicate and V5b's shared `conflicting_seqs` fork set; no second file pass (ruling 13-V4-d5). Computed separately from the engine's `coverage` on purpose: `coverage` keeps forked `seq`s whose candidates include a trusted one (point 2 still checks every distinct `(seq, hash)` candidate once), while staleness fails closed at every forked `seq` (ruling 13-V5-d2) so the "newest trusted checkpoint" anchors a single `(path, line, hash)` location.
- "Now" is read once per verify call from the system clock through a crate-private `#[cfg(test)]` clock seam on `Verifier`. Age is `now - time` on the record's signed `time` field in nanoseconds with checked (saturating) arithmetic: a future `time` saturates to age 0 and is not stale (clock problems are the business of `ClockWentBackwards`).
- Detail grammars:
  - stale: `newest trusted checkpoint seq <n> time <t> is older than max_age <d> at <now>` at the checkpoint's first copy;
  - no trusted: `no trusted checkpoint (max_age <d>)` with no file/line and no `seq`.
- `max_age` is always `Ok` at this head. Combining it with `end_before` yields `InvalidConfig` from whichever call comes second (ADR-0005 §19.2; #13 V6). `Duration::ZERO` is accepted, and a second `max_age` call replaces the first.
- Rustdoc on `FindingKind::StaleHead` states the rules, both detail grammars and both locations. CHANGELOG `Unreleased` adds one entry.
