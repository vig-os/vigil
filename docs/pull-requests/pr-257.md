# [PR 257](https://github.com/vig-os/vigil/pull/257) feat(audit): add live receipt reconciliation

## Summary

Adds `Verifier::receipts_live(bool) -> Result<Self, AuditError>` for the running-trail case where some records are in-flight (ADR-0005 decision 5 "Live trails"; ruling 13-V7-d1 point 10). With `receipts_live(true)`, reconciliation turns on, the checked range ends at the longest fully reconciled prefix (the cut), and everything above it collapses to one `Unreconciled` (info) at the first record above the cut with detail `records seq <a>..=<head> not reconciled` -- instead of a `NoReceipt` per record. Receipts above the cut are still matched, so a wrong hash or a receipt above the head stays `ReceiptMismatch`.

The cut is `min(first unreconciled seq, highest receipt seq + 1)`, dropping to the first in-range record when there are no receipts (whole range is `Unreconciled`). Checkpoints need no receipt and never break the prefix; waivers and a matched `from` extend the prefix through their records.

Conflict with `end_before` is `InvalidConfig` raised by whichever of the two calls comes second (ADR-0005 §19.2); `receipts_live(false)` is the stopped-mode default and does not conflict. A second `receipts_live` call replaces the first.

The non-test diff is confined to `src/audit/mod.rs` (one rustdoc), `src/audit/verify/mod.rs` (the builder, the field and the two conflict checks) and `src/audit/verify/receipts.rs` (the one cut computation in the existing NoReceipt loop); the reader is untouched.
