# [PR 257](https://github.com/vig-os/vigil/pull/257) feat(audit): add live receipt reconciliation

## Summary

Adds `Verifier::receipts_live(bool) -> Result<Self, AuditError>` for the running-trail case where some records are in-flight (ADR-0005 decision 5 "Live trails"; ruling 13-V7-d1 point 10 and its clarification 13-V7-d1b). With `receipts_live(true)`, reconciliation turns on, the checked range ends at the longest fully reconciled prefix (the cut), and everything above it collapses to one `Unreconciled` (info) at the first record above the cut with detail `records seq <a>..=<head> not reconciled` -- instead of a `NoReceipt` per record. Receipts above the cut are still matched, so a wrong hash or a receipt above the head stays `ReceiptMismatch`.

The cut is bounded by both the first unreconciled record AND the highest matched reconciliation evidence -- matched receipts, matched waivers and a matched `from` (clarification 13-V7-d1b point 1). A mismatched waiver or `from` is one `ReceiptMismatch` and does not count toward the cap (same ruling, point 4). The comparison is `seq <= highest evidence`, so a receipt at `u64::MAX` cannot wrap or panic (clarification 13-V7-d1b point 3). With no matched evidence, the cut drops to the first in-range record and the whole range is one `Unreconciled` (decision 5). Checkpoints need no receipt and never break the prefix; a checkpoint above the highest matched evidence is `Unreconciled` (clarification 13-V7-d1b point 2), and the operator can include the checkpoint receipts that `AuditTrail::close()` and `AuditTrail::checkpoint()` return to extend the cap through it.

Conflict with `end_before` is `InvalidConfig` raised by whichever of the two calls comes second (ADR-0005 §19.2); `receipts_live(false)` with `end_before` is accepted in either order, because §19.2 reserves a conflict for values that are themselves invalid or conflicting (as `max_age` conflicts with `end_before` for any duration), and the stopped-mode default conflicts with nothing. A second `receipts_live` call replaces the first.

The non-test diff is confined to `src/audit/verify/mod.rs` (the builder, the field and the two conflict checks), `src/audit/verify/receipts.rs` (the one cut computation in the existing `NoReceipt` loop, the matched-evidence maximum and the overflow-safe comparison) and the `CHANGELOG`; the reader is untouched.
