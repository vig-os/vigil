### Consistency sweep (`grep -n` over the ADR)

Changed: `InvalidField` (decision 4 framing and table rows), `below 0x20`, `1 000 000`/`1,000,000` (verifier side), `TrustPolicy::new`, `io::Result`, `io::Error` (decision 11 "Library", "Errors versus findings"), `seq 0` (decision 11, 18.1), `genesis` (decision 5, probe text, R10), `h+1` (decision 5 gap sentence), `R10` (amendment 9 part 2).

Left unchanged, and why:

- `InvalidField` at the `observedTimeUnixNano`, `severityNumber`/`severityText`, `body`, JSON-number, duplicate-key, uppercase-hex, reserved-key, zero-MMR, empty-key, KeyValue, nesting rows, and the "Fake records" tests: all are record-level findings on a line that decodes to an object (ruling B step 3). The nesting row's "or Unparsable at parser guard" already agrees: a parser failure is a decode failure.
- `1 000 000` in decision 6 step 2 and `TooLarge` (decision 6, Alternatives, decision 13 `AuditErrorKind`): the writer error, which 13-d2 B leaves unchanged.
- "Unescaped bytes below 0x20 are refused" (decision 4): names no kind.
- `io::Error` in decision 11's mention of "Locking" and the retry: none; the other hits are the amended sentences.
- `seq 0` in 18.3 residual rows, 18.6 history rows, 18.5, the "no start" test item (`SeqGap` for `0..k−1`) and the Collector-copy rows: ordinary prefix deletion, not a genesis resume.
- `genesis` in decision 8 (create, sealing step 3, promotion rules, the genesis value for the first segment) and amendment 9 part 1 and R2'': they already state the genesis head and base; this amendment adds only the gap and the verifier findings.
- `h+1` in the rotation text (decision 9, outcomes (ii)/(iii)): that is `h+1` of a rotation record, not a resume gap.
- An empty or whitespace-only line: decision 5 already says it is `Unparsable`; the definition cites it, so step 1 does not treat it as a fragment.

### Notes for the architect

- 13-d1 OQ11 gives `checkpoints(dir) -> io::Result<…>`; 13-d2 D's single-error-type argument is stated for the verify entry points only. I followed OQ11 literally. If `checkpoints` should return `Result<_, AuditError>`, it is a one-line change in decision 13 and §19.7.
- The accessors' return types (`Option<&Finding>`, `Option<FindingKind>`, `Option<[u8; 32]>`) are those of the plan's OQ6 text, which the ruling adopts; the ruling itself names only `first_seq`'s type.
- `TrailTrust` is documented with a `build()` so the builder chain can end; 13-d1 lists `.build()` without naming its receiver.
- The commit carries one `Refs: #13, #143` line, because the `validate commit message` hook allows only one `Refs` line.
- Deferrals: each part names its implementing slice (13-R, V1, V3 and later, C1, C2, R10) in §19; no new issue is needed.
